\begin{IEEEbiography}[{\includegraphics[width=1in,height=1.25in,clip,keepaspectratio]{tbt.jpg}}]{Hiroaki Tobita}
received the M.S. degree from the Graduate School of Information Systems, The University of Electro-Communications, Tokyo, Japan, in 1999, and the Ph.D. degree in engineering from the same university in 2004.

He was with Sony Corporation from 1999 to 2003 and with Sony Computer Science Laboratories, Tokyo, from 2003 to 2015, including a stay at Sony Computer Science Laboratory Paris from 2011 to 2013. Since 2014, he has been with the Advanced Institute of Industrial Technology, Tokyo, where he is currently a Professor. His research interests include human--computer interaction.

Dr. Tobita is a member of ACM and the Information Processing Society of Japan.
\end{IEEEbiography}
